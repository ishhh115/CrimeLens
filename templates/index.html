02 // Architecture

System Ingestion & Modeling Pipeline

The software pipeline executing ingestion, feature construction, classification, and report generation.

Stage 1 Ingestion

NCRB Records Ingestion

Extracts raw state/district crime sheets (2001–2014) from CSVs. Columns are aligned dynamically via Pandas.

Stage 2 Engineering

Feature Construction

Calculates YoY growth rates, violent ratio (murder+rape+robbery), and crime rate per lakh based on mapping populations.

Stage 3 Modeling

Random Forest Classifier

Fits 100 decision trees via Scikit-Learn to output categorical safety risk scores and feature contribution matrices.

Stage 4 Execution

Operational Outputs

Outputs normalized risk states, flags statistical z-score YoY anomalies, and writes print-ready safety briefs.

08 // System Integrity

Model Specifications & Limitations

Outlining dataset constraints and analytical limitations to ensure credibility.

Correlation vs. Causation The predictions outputted by the Random Forest classifier represent statistical associations based on reporting volumes. They do not model local causal mechanisms.
Aggregation Bias State-level features average out regional variance. Pockets of high incident rates inside districts are masked under state-level classification scores.
Historical Reporting Fluctuations Calculations represent reported cases. Fluctuations in reporting rates, public outreach, or penal registration policies heavily bias absolute volumes.
Static Modeling Scope The system models long-term offline trends and cannot account for real-time incidents or sudden administrative and legal reformations.
