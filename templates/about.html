About CrimeLens

CrimeLens is a data intelligence website that explores Indian NCRB crime records, visualizes long-term trends, and predicts state-level risk categories using a machine learning model.

What This Website Includes

  • State-wise and year-wise interactive crime analytics
  • Crime type and women-safety trend visualization
  • Random Forest risk prediction per state
  • Simple, decision-friendly risk levels: Low, Medium, High, Critical

Technology Stack

Flask, Pandas, Scikit-learn, HTML/CSS, and Chart.js.
